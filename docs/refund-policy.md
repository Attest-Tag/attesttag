# Refund policy

## Eligibility
Customers on monthly plans can request a full refund within 14 days of the first charge. Annual plans can be refunded pro-rata within 30 days. Refunds are not offered for usage-based overage charges.

## How to process a refund
1. Confirm the request came from the account owner (check the billing email in the admin panel).
2. Open the customer in Stripe and issue the refund against the original payment; never send money by bank transfer.
3. Post a note in #accounting with the customer name, amount and reason.
4. Reply to the customer within one business day.

## Escalation
Refunds above 2,000 USD need approval from the Head of Finance before they are issued.
